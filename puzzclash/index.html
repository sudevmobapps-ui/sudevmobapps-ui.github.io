PuzzClash

Same board. Same clock. The optimal solution shown at the end.

A match is three rounds, one of each kind, and whoever takes two takes the match. Connect the path, hit the target number, complete the pattern.

What makes it fair

  • Both players get the identical board and the identical clock.
  • Every round ends by showing the solver's own best line, so you can check the result.
  • No power-ups, no paid hints, no energy timers, and nothing you can buy that helps you win.
