The game

PuzzClash is free to play. You can buy ad removal, and coins for cosmetics - board themes, avatars and victory flourishes. Coins are also earned by playing. Nothing sold affects a match: there are no power-ups, no paid hints and no paid advantage of any kind.

Fair play

Rounds are timed and checked on the server. Automating play, tampering with the app, or using more than one account to influence ratings may cost you your account and your rating.

Your account

An account is created on your device and is not tied to an email address, so it cannot be recovered if the app is removed. A remove-ads purchase can be restored from the store. You may delete your account at any time.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple or Google under their own terms, and refunds are handled by them.

Availability

The game is offered as it is. Matches need a connection; training does not. Service may be interrupted for maintenance.

PuzzClash ücretsizdir. Reklam kaldırmayı ve kozmetikler için jeton satın alabilirsiniz: tahta temaları, avatarlar ve zafer efektleri. Jetonlar oynayarak da kazanılır. Satılan hiçbir şey maçı etkilemez: güçlendirme, ücretli ipucu ve herhangi bir ücretli avantaj yoktur.

Adil oyun

Raundların süresi ve doğruluğu sunucuda ölçülür. Oyunu otomatikleştirmek, uygulamayı kurcalamak veya puanları etkilemek için birden fazla hesap kullanmak hesabınıza ve puanınıza mal olabilir.
